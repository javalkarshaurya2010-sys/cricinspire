---
title: What is a wicket, really?
author: CricInspire team
type: explainer
date: 2026-08-10
summary: The word means three different things. Here is how to tell them apart.
---
A wicket can mean the three stumps and two bails the batter defends, the moment a batter is out, or the strip of ground the game is played on. Context tells you which one.
