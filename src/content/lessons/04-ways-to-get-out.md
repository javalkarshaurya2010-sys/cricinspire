When a batter is out, the team says a **wicket** has fallen. Here are the most common ways.

- **Bowled.** The ball hits the stumps and knocks off the bails.
- **Caught.** A fielder catches a hit ball before it bounces.
- **LBW (leg before wicket).** The ball hits the batter's leg or pad when it would have hit the stumps.
- **Run out.** The batter is short of the crease when a fielder breaks the stumps with the ball.
- **Stumped.** The wicketkeeper breaks the stumps while the batter is out of the crease.
- **Hit wicket.** The batter knocks their own stumps with the bat or body.

A team's innings ends when 10 batters are out, or when the overs run out in limited-overs cricket. Only 10 wickets are needed because the last batter has no partner to run with.
