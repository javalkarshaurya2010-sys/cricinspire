Cricket comes in several formats. The rules stay mostly the same. Only the length changes.

**T20.** Each team bats for one innings of up to 20 overs. A match takes about three hours, which makes it the easiest to watch and the format coming to the Olympics at LA28. Major League Cricket in the US is played in this format.

**ODI (One Day International).** Each team gets 50 overs. A match takes most of a day.

**Test cricket.** The oldest and longest format. Each team bats twice, and a match can last up to five days. Draws are possible. Fans love it for the slow build of tension.

If you are new, start with T20. It has the most action in the least time.
