Runs are the points in cricket. There are two main ways to score them.

**Running.** The two batters swap ends of the pitch. Every time both reach the other end safely, that is one run. They can run one, two, or three times before the fielders return the ball.

**Boundaries.** If the ball crosses the boundary rope after touching the ground, that is **4 runs** with no running needed. If it crosses the rope on the full, without bouncing, that is **6 runs**. Think of it like a home run, except batters also score four for a ball that rolls to the rope.

**Extras.** The batting team also gets runs when the bowling team makes mistakes. A **wide** is a ball too far from the batter to hit. A **no-ball** is an illegal delivery. A **bye** is a run scored when the ball passes the batter and the keeper. Extras go on the team total, not the batter.

**Overs.** Bowlers bowl in sets of six legal balls called an **over**. After each over, a different bowler takes over from the other end.
