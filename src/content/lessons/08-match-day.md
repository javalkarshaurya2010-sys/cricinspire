Here is how a typical T20 match plays out.

1. **The toss.** The two captains flip a coin. The winner chooses to bat or to bowl first.
2. **First innings.** One team bats for up to 20 overs, or until 10 wickets fall. Their total becomes the **target**.
3. **A short break.** The teams swap roles, usually after about 15 to 20 minutes.
4. **Second innings (the chase).** The other team tries to beat the target within their 20 overs.
5. **The result.** If the chasing team passes the target, they win. If they run out of overs or wickets first, the team that batted first wins. Ties are possible and are often settled by a one-over decider called a super over.

Two umpires on the field make the decisions. Most big matches also have a third umpire who reviews close calls on video.

That is it. You now know enough to follow a match. Next step: find a team near you, or watch a game.
