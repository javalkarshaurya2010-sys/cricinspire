You do not need much to try cricket. Many clubs lend gear to beginners, so ask before you buy.

**Bat.** Flat on one side, made of willow wood. Pick a size that fits your height. A bat that is too big is hard to control.

**Ball.** Adult matches use a hard leather ball, red for longer formats and white for limited-overs. Younger players often start with softer balls, such as tennis balls or lighter junior balls.

**Protective gear for batters.** Pads for the lower legs, gloves, a helmet, and a protective guard. When facing a hard ball, a helmet is a must.

**Wicketkeeper gear.** Special gloves and leg pads.

**Clothes.** Comfortable sports clothes and cleats or sneakers. Whites are only required in some formats.

Ask your coach or club what sizes and brands they recommend before you spend money.
